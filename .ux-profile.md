# HelloCircle UX profile

The VectorLab UX skills (`ux-audit` and the domain skills) read this file. These are owner decisions made on 2026-10-20 and
earlier. Where a VectorLab default disagrees, this file wins.

## Scope
- Workspaces: Admin `/admin`, Partner `/vendor*`, Host `/manage*` (shared `WorkspaceShell`, styles in
  `client/src/components/admin/admin.css`). The public site has its own style in `client/src/index.css`.

## Aesthetic
- Flat workspace on a light cream background (`#FBF9F4`). Very few cards and no white boxes around sections:
  sections are separated by thin dividers.
- The 70/30 record layout: the context column has no background of its own, only a thin divider line.
- List tables keep a faint 1px frame (`--adm-frame`) and row hairlines. "Flat" never means a table with no frame.
- Primary buttons are near-black (`--adm-primary`) for contrast; orange is an accent only.
- Icon tiles are coloured by meaning (`--tone-*`: green money, blue dates, amber waiting, violet people, rose
  problems, neutral reference).

## Typography
- Headings and key numbers: Bricolage Grotesque. Body text in workspaces: Inter. Public site: Figtree.
- Weights: 400 / 600 / 700.
- Minimum text size: 12px is the target. The badge, count and avatar text that's still under 12px was left
  as it is by the owner ("Not now", 2026-10-20).

## Accessibility
- WCAG 2.2 AA. Tap targets: at least 24px everywhere (2.5.8); the ⋯ menu button is 44px on touch screens.
  Making every control 44px on touch screens was offered on 2026-10-20 and not approved.

## Surfaces (overrides the VectorLab default)
- **No sliding side panels** for records, forms or decisions (project rule, CLAUDE.md). Short tasks use a centred
  popup (`AdminModal` / `ConfirmDialog`); records and longer tasks are pages (`?record=<type>:<id>`).
- Destructive actions: in ⋯ menus, confirmed in a dialog that names the thing, with the action on the button
  ("Delete listing", not "Delete"), and focus starting on Cancel.

## Forms
- Errors appear when the person leaves a field (never while typing) and clear as soon as they correct it.
  Save shows a linked summary of every problem.
- Listing-editor popups and the new-programme form keep unsaved edits as a draft in this browser and offer
  "Restore changes / Discard".

## Voice
- Sentence case, verb-first buttons; errors say what to do next and never blame the person.

## Motion
- Transform and opacity only, 100–300ms, ease-out; `prefers-reduced-motion` respected. The sidebar collapses
  instantly (no width animation).
